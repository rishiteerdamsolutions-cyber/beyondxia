HOW IT IS BUILT

pRAG is the core. Kinkar is the first product on it.

  1. 1

    pRAG core

    Index documents the desk is allowed to use. Retrieve matching passages. Claude answers with page references, or explains aloud.

  2. 2

    Staffing material

    Kinkar is that same core, pointed at US IT staffing: job descriptions, client requirements, process notes, and candidate material you have permission to use.

  3. 3

    Recruiter questions

    Ask what the req actually requires, how a profile lines up, or what to brief the client — and open the source pages instead of trusting a summary.

  4. 4

    Human desk stays in charge

    Kinkar does not place candidates or make hiring decisions. Recruiters check the references and own the outreach.

WHO IT IS FOR

US IT staffing desks that already work from documents

FOR RECRUITERS

Ground every answer in the req

Must-haves, nice-to-haves, location, rate, and client quirks stay attached to the pages they came from — so a new recruiter on the desk is not guessing.

FOR THE FIRM

Your material, not a public scrape

Kinkar does not bundle a candidate database or scrape job boards. You add what you are allowed to use. Claude answers only from that set.

HONEST LIMITS

What Kinkar is not

Not a job board or ATS replacement. It does not post jobs, parse every resume in the market, or auto-submit candidates.

Not a substitute for permission. Only load job descriptions, resumes, and notes you have the right to use. Candidate data is the desk’s responsibility.

Not always right. Answers can miss a constraint. Open the referenced pages before you tell a client or a candidate anything that matters.
