PRIVACY POLICY

How pRAG handles your data

Effective 10 October 2026 · BEYONDX INFORMATICS ANALYTICS PRIVATE LIMITED (CIN: U62013TS2023PTC173659), Flat No: 9-6-192, Ram Nagar, Karimnagar, Telangana, 505001, India.

Your documents stay on your computer

pRAG is designed to run locally. The PDFs you add are loaded and indexed on your own device, and we (Beyondx) do not receive, store, or see your documents or your API key.

What goes to Anthropic

To generate answers, pRAG sends your question plus the relevant retrieved passages to Anthropic's Claude API, using the API key you provide. That traffic is between you and Anthropic under your own account — please review Anthropic's privacy policy and terms to understand how they handle it.

What we receive

Only what you choose to send us: messages you write on WhatsApp (+91 95050 09699), emails to ceo@beyondxia.xyz, or anything you share when requesting access or support. We use it solely to respond and improve pRAG and Kinkar, and we don't sell it.

Kinkar

Kinkar is the first product built on pRAG. The same rules apply: documents you add stay under your management; questions and retrieved passages go to the Claude API with the key you provide. Staffing desks are responsible for having permission to use job descriptions, resumes, and candidate notes they load.

Source material rules

Only use documents you own, that are openly licensed, or that you have permission to use (for example, material your school has authorised, or staffing documents your desk is allowed to process). pRAG and Kinkar do not bundle, distribute, or sell textbooks, candidate databases, or study material.

Students and minors

Schools, tutors, and parents deploying pRAG for students are responsible for document permissions and for supervising use, including API-key management. Students should never handle billing keys themselves.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
