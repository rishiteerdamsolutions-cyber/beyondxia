HOW IT WORKS

From PDFs to answers in four steps

  1. 1

    Add your PDFs

    Load chapters or notes you have permission to use. Nothing is bundled in — your material, your syllabus.

  2. 2

    Connect your Claude key

    Get an API key from Anthropic's console, paste it into pRAG. Bring-your-own-key: your account, your usage, your control.

  3. 3

    Build your knowledge base

    pRAG indexes your documents so Claude can match each question to the right pages.

  4. 4

    Ask, read, or listen

    Ask questions and get answers with references — or press play and let voice mode explain like a real person.

TWO MODES

Answers with proof. Explanations with voice.

PRIVACY & SOURCE MATERIAL

Plain words on data

You provide everything. Your documents and your Claude API key stay under your management — we don't bundle, distribute, or sell textbooks or study material.

Only upload what you're allowed to use. Use PDFs you own, that are openly licensed, or that your school has authorised. Don't upload pirated or borrowed material.

Answers involve Anthropic's Claude API. Because you supply your own key, your questions and the retrieved passages are sent to Anthropic to generate answers. Check Anthropic's terms to understand how they handle that data.

An assistant, not an authority. pRAG helps you work through your own material. It can make mistakes — verify important answers against the source pages.

FAQ

Questions, answered carefully

Do I need to bring my own PDFs?

Yes. pRAG ships with no built-in library — you add the chapters and notes you have permission to use, and everything it teaches comes from that material.

Which AI provider does pRAG use?

Claude. pRAG is made with Claude AI models and powered by the Claude API (Anthropic). You connect your own Anthropic API key — see the documentation for setup steps.

Does it show where an answer came from?

Yes. Every answer lists the pages it draws on, and you can expand each reference to read the exact passage — so you can always verify.

Can it explain a whole document?

Yes. Voice mode works through a document section by section, explaining each part in plain words. Very long documents may be covered across multiple sessions.

Does it work without a Claude API key?

No — a key is required for generating answers and lessons. Adding and organising your documents can be done first; the docs explain each step.
